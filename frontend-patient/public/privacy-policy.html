What we collect

  • Phone number — used to create your account and verify identity via a one-time SMS code. Stored encrypted at rest.
  • Location data — collected only when you use location-dependent features (finding nearby hospitals, or during an active emergency dispatch request), and only after you grant permission through your device's standard permission prompt. Not collected in the background outside of an active dispatch.
  • Symptoms you enter during a triage session, and the resulting suggested conditions and urgency level.
  • Device identifier — a per-install identifier (not your device's IMEI or advertising ID) used to detect abuse of the emergency-dispatch system, such as many false requests filed from the same device under different phone numbers.
  • Dispatch request records — if you request emergency transport, we keep a record of the request, its outcome, and related verification steps (e.g. a callback from a dispatcher), both to coordinate your care and to improve abuse detection.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your data to third parties.
  • We do not use your symptom or location data to serve you advertising.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or other apps' data.
