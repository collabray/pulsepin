Not a substitute for emergency services

PulsePin is a symptom-triage and hospital-routing tool. It is not a certified medical device and does not replace professional medical judgment. If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact your local emergency services number directly — do not rely solely on this app.

Account & verification

You must provide a phone number you control to create an account and verify it via a one-time SMS code. You're responsible for keeping access to that number and to your account secure.

Emergency dispatch requests

Requesting emergency transport through the app does not guarantee automatic dispatch. Requests are scored for plausibility and, in most cases, confirmed by a human dispatcher — including a direct callback — before a vehicle is sent, consistent with how comparable emergency-request platforms operate.
